# Wishes

## What it does
Members post wishes for activities they would like to join. `list_wishes` lists every wish, including archived ones, newest first. A member archives one of their wishes with `archive_wish`; an archived wish stays archived.

## Entry points
- `App.Wishes.create_wish/1`
- `App.Wishes.list_wishes/1`
- `App.Wishes.archive_wish/2`
